                     PROPOSED FLOW CHART / PROCESS FLOW
====================================================================================================

Dokumento para sa Thesis / Capstone System Architecture:
Ito ang opisyal at pinakabagong Process Flow para sa tatlong (3) uri ng users:
  1. STUDENT PROCESS FLOW (CWTS Student Volunteer)
  2. COMMUNITY PARTNER PROCESS FLOW (Barangay / NGO Official)
  3. CWTS COORDINATOR PROCESS FLOW (Administrator / Unit Staff)

Naka-istruktura sa multi-column layout gamit ang On-Page Circular Connectors (B, C, D)
alinsunod sa standard thesis manuscript format na ipinakita sa reference images.

Batay sa aktuwal na mga modules ng system:
  STUDENT MODULES:
    - Main: Dashboard, My Profile, CMO Requirements
    - Monitoring: Deployment & Monitoring, Feedback & Evaluation
    * Tandaan: Ang Community Official ang naglo-log ng attendance ng mga estudyante.
    * Walang make-up duty module ang student.

  COMMUNITY PARTNER MODULES:
    - Main: Dashboard, Community Requests
    - Monitoring: Deployment & Monitoring, Attendance & Hours, Feedback & Evaluation
    * Tandaan: Ang Community Official ang naglo-log ng attendance (Time-In & Time-Out) at nag-e-evaluate ng estudyante.

```mermaid
flowchart TB
    %% ================= COLUMN 1 =================
    subgraph Col1 ["1. Authentication & Setup"]
        direction TB
        Start([Start]) --> SignUp[Sign Up<br>Student Number & Info]
        SignUp --> VerifyOTP[Verify Email OTP]
        VerifyOTP --> Login[Login to Student Portal]
        Login --> B_out((B))
    end

    %% ================= COLUMN 2 =================
    subgraph Col2 ["2. Profile & Requirements"]
        direction TB
        B_in((B)) --> Dashboard[Dashboard<br>Status & Announcements]
        Dashboard --> MyProfile[My Profile<br>Info, Map Location, & Skills]
        MyProfile --> UploadCMO[CMO Requirements<br>Upload Waiver, Medical, Valid ID]
        UploadCMO --> DecCMO{Are documents<br>approved by<br>Coordinator?}
        DecCMO -- No --> ReuploadDocs[Update / Re-upload<br>Required Documents]
        ReuploadDocs --> UploadCMO
        DecCMO -- Yes --> C_out((C))
    end

    %% ================= COLUMN 3 =================
    subgraph Col3 ["3. Deployment & Monitoring"]
        direction TB
        C_in((C)) --> WaitDSS[Wait for DSS<br>Auto-Matching]
        WaitDSS --> ViewDeploy[Deployment & Monitoring<br>View Site & Schedule]
        ViewDeploy --> AttendFieldwork[Attend Community<br>Fieldwork Activities]
        AttendFieldwork --> OfficialLogs[Community Official<br>Logs Attendance<br>Time-In & Time-Out]
        OfficialLogs --> MonitorHours[Monitor Rendered Hours<br>& Service Logs]
        MonitorHours --> D_out((D))
    end

    %% ================= COLUMN 4 =================
    subgraph Col4 ["4. Evaluation & Completion"]
        direction TB
        D_in((D)) --> FeedbackEval[Feedback & Evaluation<br>Rate Site & Supervisor]
        FeedbackEval --> TrackHours[Track Rendered NOH<br>Confirm 54 Hours Met]
        TrackHours --> EditProfile[Edit Profile & Settings]
        EditProfile --> ChangePass[Change Password]
        ChangePass --> Logout[Logout]
        Logout --> EndStudent([End])
    end

    %% FORCE EXACT TOP HORIZONTAL ROW ALIGNMENT ACROSS ALL COLUMNS
    Start ~~~ B_in
    B_in ~~~ C_in
    C_in ~~~ D_in

    %% STRICT LEFT-TO-RIGHT COLUMN ORDER CONSTRAINTS
    Col1 ~~~ Col2
    Col2 ~~~ Col3
    Col3 ~~~ Col4

    %% STYLING TO KEEP CIRCLES AND BOXES CLEAN
    classDef default fill:#ffffff,stroke:#0f172a,stroke-width:1.5px,font-family:Arial;
```

```mermaid
flowchart TB
    %% ================= COLUMN 1 =================
    subgraph Col1 ["1. Authentication & Overview"]
        direction TB
        StartCoord([Start]) --> LoginCoord[Login to Coordinator Portal]
        LoginCoord --> MainDashboard[Dashboard<br>KPI Stats, Charts & Realtime Overview]
        MainDashboard --> B_out((B))
    end

    %% ================= COLUMN 2 =================
    subgraph Col2 ["2. User & CMO Compliance"]
        direction TB
        B_in((B)) --> UserMgmt[User Management<br>Students & Community Partners]
        UserMgmt --> CMOCompliance[CMO Compliance<br>Audit Waivers, Medicals, Valid IDs]
        CMOCompliance --> DecCMO{Are documents<br>compliant and<br>approved?}
        DecCMO -- No --> RejectCMO[Flag with Remarks<br>for Resubmission]
        DecCMO -- Yes --> MarkCompliant[Mark Student Compliant<br>& Eligible for Deployment]
        MarkCompliant --> C_out((C))
    end

    %% ================= COLUMN 3 =================
    subgraph Col3 ["3. Requests & DSS Deployment"]
        direction TB
        C_in((C)) --> ReviewRequests[Community Requests<br>Review Partner Proposals]
        ReviewRequests --> DecReq{Is project<br>request<br>qualified?}
        DecReq -- No --> ReviseReqCoord[Reject or Request<br>Revisions to Partner]
        DecReq -- Yes --> ApproveReqCoord[Approve Project &<br>Open Volunteer Slots]
        ApproveReqCoord --> RunDSSCoord[DSS Matching Algorithm<br>Proximity, Skills & Schedule]
        RunDSSCoord --> DeploymentMgmt[Deployment Management<br>Finalize & Publish Batches]
        DeploymentMgmt --> D_out((D))
    end

    %% ================= COLUMN 4 =================
    subgraph Col4 ["4. Audit & Report Generation"]
        direction TB
        D_in((D)) --> AuditAttendance[Attendance Logs<br>Audit DTR & Site Logs]
        AuditAttendance --> RenderedHours[Rendered Hours<br>Validate 54-Hr Completion]
        RenderedHours --> FeedbackEvalCoord[Feedback & Evaluation<br>Audit 360-Degree Ratings]
        FeedbackEvalCoord --> ReportGen[Report Generation<br>Generate CHED CMO Reports]
        ReportGen --> LogoutCoord[Logout]
        LogoutCoord --> EndCoord([End])
    end
```
