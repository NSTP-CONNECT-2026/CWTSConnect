Intelligent Community Needs Matching & Student Deployment

CWTSConnect is a dedicated academic platform designed to streamline NSTP-CWTS operations. It coordinates barangay project requests, evaluates student skill compatibility using a Decision Support System (DSS), and monitors field attendance and CMO compliance.

Decision Support System

Simple Additive Weighting (SAW) Method

To prevent arbitrary student placement and ensure students are assigned where their skills and location provide the greatest community benefit, CWTSConnect employs the Simple Additive Weighting (SAW) multi-criteria decision-making model.

Weight: 40%
Course & Skill Match
Alignment between student academic background and barangay project requirements.
Weight: 30%
Proximity & Travel Distance
Geographic closeness of student residence to the deployment site.
Weight: 20%
Schedule Availability
Compatibility between project operating schedule and student availability.
Weight: 10%
Service Track Record
Prior civic involvement and demonstrated readiness.
Computational Pipeline
1. Criteria Matrix Formulation Step 1
Quantifies student attributes (skills, location coordinates, schedule) against project requirements.
2. Value Normalization Step 2
Scales all measurement criteria into comparable non-dimensional values between 0 and 1.
3. Weighted Aggregation Step 3
Calculates total preference score: Vi = ∑ (wj × rij) for each eligible candidate.
4. Ranked Recommendations Step 4
Presents sorted recommendations to the NSTP Coordinator for final deployment authorization.

Core Management Capabilities

Tracks parent consent waivers, medical clearances, and student documents to ensure every deployed student meets CHED CMO requirements.

Process Flow

Deployment Lifecycle

The sequential administrative procedure governing student deployment from request initiation to completion certification.

01

Request Submission

Barangay officials file community service requests specifying project focus, location, schedule, and volunteer headcount.

02

Student Clearance

Students complete profiles and submit required CMO parent consent waivers and medical certificates for verification.

03

DSS Matching

The SAW algorithm calculates suitability rankings; the NSTP Coordinator reviews recommendations and confirms assignments.

04

Fieldwork & Evaluation

Students render community service with verified attendance, followed by project evaluation and completion sign-off.

Support & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Standard guidance regarding system usage, student obligations, and institutional compliance policies.

The DSS applies the Simple Additive Weighting (SAW) algorithm to objectively match student academic capabilities, location proximity, and availability with barangay needs. This ensures effective community service delivery while minimizing student travel risks.
Under CHED Memorandum Orders and NSTP regulations, all student trainees must upload a signed Parent/Guardian Consent Waiver, Medical Certificate of Physical Fitness, and Emergency Contact details before they can be deployed for off-campus community service.
Attendance records are logged during fieldwork sessions. Accrued hours are displayed in the student dashboard. In cases of valid absences, students may submit a formal Make-Up Service Request through the system for coordinator review and approval.
Barangay officials use registered institutional credentials provided by the NSTP Office. Upon login, officials can file new community requests, inspect assigned student batches, and complete post-activity evaluations.
